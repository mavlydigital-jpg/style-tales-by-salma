Welcome to Style Tales

Style Tales by Salma is a beauty shop on Ngong Road in Kilimani, inside The Greenhouse Mall. We do hair, nails, waxing and massage, all under one roof.

Our space is calm, clean and welcoming. Bring your kids, bring your friends, or come alone and take an hour for you. Every visit is gentle, careful and done with love.
